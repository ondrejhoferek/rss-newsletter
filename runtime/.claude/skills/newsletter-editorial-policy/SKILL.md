# Newsletter Editorial Policy

When making editorial decisions for the newsletter:

1. **Relevance over recency** — A highly relevant article from 2 days ago beats a marginally relevant one from today.
2. **Source diversity** — No more than 2 items from the same source in a single newsletter.
3. **Actionability** — Prefer articles that help the reader do something (learn, build, decide) over pure announcements.
4. **Signal over noise** — Skip marketing fluff, minor version bumps, and repackaged press releases.
5. **Honest limitations** — If an excerpt is too short to judge quality, flag it rather than guessing.
