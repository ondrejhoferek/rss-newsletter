# Newsletter Quality Rules

- Prefer diversity of sources — avoid selecting multiple items from the same feed.
- Rank practical, actionable content higher than announcements without substance.
- Penalize clickbait titles and low-information marketing content.
- When summarizing, lead with what changed or what's new, not background context.
- "Why it matters" should be specific to the reader's interests, not generic.
- If an excerpt is too short to assess quality, note the limitation in warnings.
